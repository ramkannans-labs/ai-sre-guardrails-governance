# Trainer Guide: Week 6 - Guardrails & Governance

## Session Objective
Equip SREs to build automated governance pipelines that enforce SLO compliance, manage AI agent lifecycles, and generate audit trails for compliance.

## Session Flow (~6 Hours)

| Phase | Title | Duration | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Foundations | 30m | Governance principles, policy-based automation, and compliance frameworks. |
| 2 | Lab 6.1-6.2 | 90m | Building policies for AWS and Kubernetes resources. |
| 3 | Lab 6.3 | 60m | Configuring AgentOps and AI agent governance. |
| 4 | Lab 6.4-6.5 | 90m | Audit trails, compliance reporting, and policy enforcement. |
| 5 | Lab 6.6 | 60m | Shadow Duel: Benchmarking human vs AI governance capabilities. |
| 6 | Wrap-up | 30m | Connecting governance to business outcomes and culture. |

## Trainer Notes
- **Policy by Default**: Emphasize that every automated system has implicit governance - being explicit is better than hiding.
- **AgentOps**: Frame AgentOps as "guardrails for AI" - enabling humans to understand and control AI behavior.
- **Shadow Duel**: Position Week 6 as a reflective exercise showing where human expertise still adds value.
- **Business Impact**: Connect governance back to business costs of incidents, compliance fines, and customer trust.
